In the previous part of this series, I demonstrated that the old gods are still around and active in today's age - as they have been throughout history \cite{part3}. One of the most known and most mentioned of the old gods is Baal. Throughout scripture, Baal appears as the head deity of the Canaanites and Phoenicians and tied to agriculture, storms and rain, fertility, child sacrifice\footnote{Jeremiah 19:5 - ``And have built the high places of Baal to burn their sons in the fire as burnt offerings to Baal.''}, and political power\footnote{There is a constant theme in the scriptures of entire nations bending to and worshiping Baal. An example being Judges 2:11-13 - ``Then the children of Israel did evil in the sight of the Lord, and served the Baals'' ... ``They forsook the Lord and served Baal and the Ashtoreths.''}\cite{baal strongs}. Baal is referenced about 80 times in the old testament alone. The context is almost always that of spiritual adultery and apostasy \footnote{``An act of refusing to continue to follow, obey, or recognize a religious faith'' \cite{apostasy defn}}\cite{baal strongs}. This framing of Baal is from ancient times, though. What is Baal's role in the modern age?

The Hebrew word \textit{Baal} literally means `lord' \cite{Strong's GodRules, indiaTimes}, `master'\cite{baal strongs}, or 'owner`\cite{baal strongs, indiaTimes}. The meaning of his name essentially summarizes his role. His signature routine is that he becomes the \textit{lord} of a nation or people by \textit{replacing} God in their eyes. That pattern is evident by the scriptures and myths of how Baal interacts with the world. However, surprisingly, there is not much direct information about who or what Baal is. The scriptures certainly don't explain it - despite the mention of Baal numerous times. The idea and concept of Baal is nearly always assumed to be known and understood by the reader. When you research Baal, you find very few details. The details that are found are in the form of ancient myths, stories, and recurring patterns that have unfolded in the aftermath of his presence. 

Baal often appears throughout cultures with different names - such as Baal Peor\footnote{Deuteronomy 4:3 - ``Your eyes have seen what the LORD did at Baal Peor; for the LORD your God has destroyed from among you all the men who followed Baal of Peor.''}, Baal-Zebub\footnote{2 Kings 1:16 - ``Then he said to him, ``Thus says the LORD: ‘Because you have sent messengers to inquire of Baal-Zebub, the god of Ekron, is it because there is no God in Israel to inquire of His word? ...''}, Baal-Berith\footnote{Judges 8:33 - ``So it was, as soon as Gideon was dead, that the children of Israel again played the harlot with the Baals, and made Baal-Berith their god.''}, and many more. He is both spoken of as one and many. This makes Baal seem almost more of a \textit{concept} rather than the surname of a single \textit{entity}. That concept would simply be that of apostasy - opposing God. There are even some less-accepted lines of thought that suggest Baal could even be Satan himself. These suggest that versions of his name, \textit{Beelzebul} are simply distortions of \textit{Beelzebub} - the king of the demons \cite{Yahweh and gods}. Regardless, he is a being who is clearly in \textit{opposition} of God - a sort of anti-God.

One of the oldest set of texts we (humans) have discovered are the Ugaritic letters and ritual texts. These texts were discovered in 1929 in modern-day Syria. These texts were written in a mix of Akkadian scripts and Semitic languages and identified to be from around the time of 1400-1200 B.C \cite{Ugaritic Letters}. These texts, if you take them at their word, reveal that Baal is actually the epithet of a single cosmic deity - despite having local, personal names for various nations. Each of those various names represent different manifestations of the same being. These texts also suggest that the Canaanites actually began equating 'Yahweh and Baal' as the same being. They convinced themselves that Baal \textit{was} Yahweh. They saw Baal as their giver of grains, wine, and oil - which placed him as the lord and owner of their nation. They baked that concept into their daily lives, their language, and even their names \cite{Yahweh and gods}. This is a large part of the context for the book of Hosea. The conflation of Yahweh and Baal was precisely what Hosea was countering:
\begin{quotation}
``For she did not know That \textit{I} gave her grain, new wine, and oil, And multiplied her silver and gold--\textit{Which they prepared for Baal}.'' - Hosea 2:8
\end{quotation}
This is the scheme of Baal. 

Does this pattern manifest itself in today's world? Has Baal convinced nations and peoples to worship him, while thinking they are worshiping the true God? Consider the modern state of Israel, of which the majority population is Jewish \cite{jewish pop}. This area was the homeland of the ancient Canaanites - whom, at times in history \textit{were} Baal worshipers. Many people believe the modern day Jews to be Gods chosen people - as they were described in the old testament by the term \textit{Israel}\footnote{Psalm 135:4 - ``For the Lord has chosen Jacob for Himself, \textit{Israel} for His special treasure.''}. In recent months, the state of Iran created a Baal Effigy marked with Israel's Star Of David, and then burned it while chanting ``Death to Israel'' and ``Death to America.'' The \textit{India Times} stated that this event appeared to equate Israel with corruption, evil, and satanic symbolism \cite{indiaTimes}. This suggests that some people think the modern nation state of Israel actually worships Baal, rather than God. The purpose of this message is not to say whether that is true or false. However, if you find that hard to believe, recall that it certainly wouldn't be the first time in History that \textit{Israel} (or rather, the \textit{Israelite's} representing God's chosen people living together as a nation in this context) fell to demon worship. The old testament records this pattern happening continually throughout history\footnote{One example is in 1 Kings 12:28-30: ``Therefore the king asked advice, made two calves of gold, and said to the people, `It is too much for you to go up to Jerusalem. Here are your gods, O Israel, which brought you up from the land of Egypt!' And he set up one in Bethel, and the other he put in Dan. Now this thing became a sin, for the people went to worship before the one as far as Dan.''}.

To be clear, if a nation or its leaders are controlled by evil, that does not mean all of its people are. This can be quickly demonstrated with the story of Sodom and Gomorrah. Lot was a righteous man\footnote{2 Peter 2:7: ``and delivered righteous Lot, who was oppressed by the filthy conduct of the wicked''} living in a lawless place\footnote{2 Peter 2:8: ``(for that righteous man, dwelling among them, tormented his righteous soul from day to day by seeing and hearing their lawless deeds)''} - a place so lawless that God destroyed it for its sin. Therefore, a nation following after Baal does not imply that all of its people are.

What would a Baal worshiping Israel mean for us in the United States? Many of our leaders hold firm that Israel is our greatest ally. Even Benjamin Netanyahu (The Prime Minister of Israel) stated that ``Donald Trump is the greatest friend that the state of Israel has ever had in the White House \cite{msn}.'' If we are a God fearing nation, would we hold a Baal fearing nation close at our side? And if we are a Baal fearing nation, would Israel hold us closely as a Godly nation? Many believe the United States to be a godly nation - a righteous places that shines over the rest of the world. There are certainly things we (as a country) do shine at - such as military power and spending (the power to kill)\cite{SIPRI}, the highest incarceration rates \cite{prisonpolicy}, the highest national debt \cite{economic comittee, traasuryDirect}, etc. These should not be the things that a Godly nation shines at above others. Are we then, a godly nation - or are we deceiving ourselves and perhaps worshiping Baal?

The United Church Of God teaches that The United States fulfills the blessings given to Joseph's son Manasseh\footnote{Genesis 48 describes Jacob blessing Joseph's sons.} \cite{UCG Prophecy} - that it has become a great nation. The Pew Research Center published that in 2007, almost 80\% of US adults identified as Christian \cite{Christianity Percent}. To Baal, a great nation which is predominately Christian is the prime target. Over the years, that percentage has been quickly declining. The United States has rejected biblical morality and continuing to turn toward Apostasy. That pattern \textit{is} the pattern of turning to Baal. By moving away from God, we are giving ownership to Baal and letting him become the lord of the land. This is the exact thing that has happened repeatedly throughout history.

The recent release of millions of Epstein Files heavily support this pattern. These files show that a large number of very powerful and highly influential elites are involved in practices which are antithetical to Gods way. These include (but are not limited to) the exploitation of children, human trafficking, political corruption, and much more; all while seemingly remaining protected and avoiding any justice. When a nation rejects God, Baal does not become the master by stepping into power. He does so by gaining authority of its institutions through leaders and people who shape society with values that oppose God.

Throughout history, when we humans decide that we want to reject biblical morality, and throw God aside, the scriptures record that as \textit{turning to Baal}. What the nations are doing today is no different. When we turn away from God by allowing corrupt elites to take control and allow ungodly forces to take over our institutions and governments, we are simply  raising up a pedestal with Baal atop it. Through the rejection of God, we are giving up our lands and institutions to Baal - making him the owner of our lands. This is no different than ancient times. This is a pattern that has repeated again and again throughout the generations and \textit{must} remain clear and obvious to us today. Therefore, when we look around and see all these things happening today, remember that is simply how the enemy works.
