\bookchapter{\Jacob Sent to His Uncle \name{Laban}}

\bverse Then \Isaac called \Jacob and blessed him, and charged him, and said to him: ``You shall not take a wife from the daughters of \location{Canaan}.
\bverse Arise, go to \location{Padan Aram}, to the house of \name{Bethuel} your mother's father; and take yourself a wife from there of the daughters of \name{Laban} your mother's brother.

\bverse ``May God Almighty bless you, And make you fruitful and multiply you, That you may be an assembly of peoples; \bverse And give you the blessing of \Abraham, To you and your descendants with you, That you may inherit the land In which you are a stranger, Which God gave to \Abraham.''
\end{bquotation}
